Use SINGLE only when ALL of the following are true:
- there is one local behavioral concern;
- at most two mutable paths are required;
- no migration/API/frontend/backend/protocol boundary needs sequencing;
- one worker can perform the change mechanically without architectural
  discovery.

- more than one independently testable behavior;
- more than two mutable files;
- cross-layer work;

- backend plus frontend;
- protocol/schema plus consumers;
- refactor followed by migration/adaptation;
- one step materially reduces the context needed by the next.

separate steps when they belong to one local behavioral change.
